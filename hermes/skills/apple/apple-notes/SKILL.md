# Apple Notes

Bryan's personal second brain is the Markdown vault `~/second-brain`, synced
only through Forgejo Git. Apple Notes held it from 2026-09-16 to 2026-10-31;
everything written there was brought back into the vault and the Notes folder
was deleted (decision:
`~/second-brain/Decisions/2026-10-31-decision-return-personal-second-brain-to-markdown-git.md`).

## What to load instead

- **`vault-pkm`** plus `~/second-brain/AGENTS.md` — personal recall and capture.
- **`knowledge-capture`** — decides at a resting point whether something is
  worth keeping and whether it belongs in `~/second-brain` or a project vault.

## Rules

1. Never write personal knowledge to Apple Notes.
2. There is no agent Notes integration: `memo`, a Notes MCP, the Notes
   database, and widened macOS permissions stay out of scope. If Bryan asks
   about a specific Apple Note, ask him to share its text.
